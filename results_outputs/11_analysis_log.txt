3. MODEL SPECIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  outcome ~ smile_type * culture + (1 | smiler_id) + (1 | stimulus)
  Crossed random intercepts, fitted with lme4::lmer.

  smile_type  within-smiler   (each person produced both types)
  culture     between-smiler
  smiler_id   random intercept - repeated smiles from one person are
              not independent, and people vary in expressiveness
  stimulus    random intercept - some videos are funnier than others

  Unit of analysis is the individual smile, NOT the person mean.
  This is deliberate: the number of smiles per person varies, and
  averaging would treat a person with one smile as being as precise
  as a person with eight.

  Duration covariate added for cumulative outcomes: _durtot _durmax _auc _neps
